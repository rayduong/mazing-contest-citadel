How to Play

New to mazing games? Here is the whole game in plain English.

THE BIG IDEA

Don't kill the runner — just waste their time!

A little runner starts at the North Gate (top) and wants to reach the South Exit (bottom) as fast as they can. You place Stone Walls on the grass to force them to take the longest, slowest walk possible. Whoever keeps their runner trapped inside their maze for the most seconds wins the round!

How Every Round Works

1
Plan for 5 Seconds

Each round starts with a 5-second look-around timer. You can't place walls yet — use this time to spot where the glowing Spires and Crimson Keeps are!

2
Build Your Maze

Tap or click open grass to place 2×2 Stone Walls from your round's Wall Supply. Watch the white path line stretch! Removing a wall refunds it for free.

3
Golden Rule: Leave a Path!

You can never completely seal off the exit. There must always be at least a 1-tile open walkway from the North Gate to the South Exit.

4
Lock In & Watch Them Run

Hit Ready / Start (or wait for the timer). Everyone's runner starts at once. Longest survival time takes 1st place!

Secret #1: Build Back-and-Forth Zig-Zags!

Think of an airport security line or theme park queue. Instead of building random blocks, make walls that stretch across the island with a small gap on alternating sides. The runner has to walk all the way left, turn around, walk all the way right, and repeat!

Tip: Keep an eye on the Est time at the top of the screen — it updates live after every wall you place!

What Are Those Objects Already on the Grass?

You don't build these — they start on the map! Your job is to bend your maze around them.

Storm Spire (Yellow)

Zaps the runner every 3 seconds and cuts their walking speed in HALF. Wrap your maze around it so it zaps them 2 or 3 times!

Frost Obelisk (Blue)

Always slows the runner while they're inside its blue circle, plus fires freezing bursts. Best when you spiral tightly around it!

Chrono Spire (Purple)

Big slow hit AND instantly speeds up the cooldown of every other Spire on your island so they fire again sooner!

Gold Waystone (Checkpoint)

A mandatory pit-stop! On rounds with a Gold Waystone, the runner must touch it first before heading to the South Exit.

Secret #2: Crimson Keeps Are Power Batteries!

See the red stone buildings (Crimson Keeps)? They act like batteries! If you connect a continuous chain of your Stone Walls touching a Crimson Keep and bringing it within 1 tile of a Spire, that Spire lights up with Overcharge — giving it bigger range and faster slow pulses!

Link up to 3 Crimson Keeps into the same wall network for maximum power (shown as glowing diamonds ◆◆◆ on the Spire).

Meet the 4 Types of Runners

Citadel Scout Normal speed, no special tricks. Easy to learn on!
Royal Outrider Runs fast on open grass, but slows last 25% longer. Keep them near Spires!
Iron Juggernaut Shrugs off part of normal slows — link a Crimson Keep to break through their armor!
Wind Herald Gets a speed burst if they go 3.2s without hitting a Spire. Don't leave huge empty gaps!

Touch & Mobile Controls

  • Tap grass or hold-drag to aim your 2×2 wall preview.
  • Use the Arrow Nudge Pad for exact 1-tile precision, then tap Place.
  • Tap an existing wall to select and remove it (+1 wall refunded).
  • Tap any Spire, Crimson Keep, or Waystone to see its range and details.
  • Pinch with two fingers to zoom, or use the Pan tool to glide around.
