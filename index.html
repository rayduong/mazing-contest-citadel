Custom Rules

1. Shape the Labyrinth

Place 2×2 Stone Pillars (100% refund when reclaimed) anywhere on the sunlit meadow. Nearby pillars automatically connect into straight and diagonal stone walls to wind runners from the North Gate to the South Shrine.

2. Route Past Elemental Spires

Fold switchbacks and spirals around map spires for repeated hits: Storm Spires unleash a 50% shock slow, Frost Obelisks chill runners inside a permafrost aura plus glacial pulses, and Chrono Spires warp time while recharging other spires.

3. Link Keeps for Overcharge

Connect pre-placed Crimson Keeps into the same wall network as an Elemental Spire to Overcharge it (up to 3 stars)—expanding spire radius, shortening cooldowns, and piercing heavy Juggernaut armor.

4. Outlast Rivals & Beat Par

Longest runner survival time wins each round, with +2 bonus points for beating the round’s Par target. Adapt your layout for swift Outriders, armored Juggernauts, and gale-hasted Wind Heralds.

How to Play

Every open grass tile inside the limestone border is buildable—craft the longest survival path!

Stone Pillars

Place 2×2 Stone Pillars (100% refund when reclaimed). Adjacent pillars connect automatically in straight, 45° diagonal, and angled steps.

Elemental Spires

Weave paths around Storm (50% shock), Frost (continuous chill + nova), and Chrono (time-warp + global spire recharge) spires.

Crimson Keep Overcharge

Link Crimson Keeps through your walls to nearby spires to grant Overcharge stars—boosting range, haste, and armor pierce.

Runners & Par

Longest survival from North Gate to South Shrine wins each round. Beat the Par target for +2 bonus points.
