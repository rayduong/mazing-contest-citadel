Room Rules

Standard Contest Rules · 8 rounds, round-scaled build timer (90s–120s), normal wall supply, and AI mason opponents.

1. Build Stone Walls

Place 2×2 Stone Walls on any open grass tile. Nearby walls connect automatically to wind the runner from the North Gate to the South Shrine and through any gilded Checkpoint. Removing walls is always free.

2. Route Past Spires

Fold switchbacks and spirals around map spires for repeated hits. Storm Spires slow by 50% (2.4s), Frost Obelisks chill by 22% inside their ring plus 45% bursts (2.2s), and Chrono Spires slow by 55% (2.0s) while cutting 1.2s off other spires.

3. Link Keeps for Overcharge

Connect red Crimson Keeps through your walls to within 1 corridor tile of a spire for up to three Overcharge tiers, boosting spire range, pulse speed, and piercing Juggernaut armor.

4. Outlast Rivals & Beat Par

Longest survival time wins each round (10 / 7 / 5 / 3 / 2 / 1 pts by rank). Keep the runner in your maze past the Par target (such as 28s+) for +2 bonus pts. Practice scaffolds via Sample Maze · Practice run unranked.
