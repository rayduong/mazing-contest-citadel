Room Rules

1. Build Stone Walls

Place 2×2 Stone Walls anywhere on open grass. Nearby blocks connect into walls automatically to wind the runner from the North Gate to the South Shrine. Removing walls is always free.

2. Route Past Spires

Fold switchbacks and spirals around map spires for repeated hits. Storm Spires slow by 50%, Frost Obelisks chill inside their ring plus 45% bursts, and Chrono Spires slow by 55% while recharging other spires.

3. Link Keeps for Overcharge

Connect red Crimson Keeps through your walls to nearby spires to grant up to three Overcharge diamonds, expanding spire range, speeding up pulses, and piercing Juggernaut armor.

4. Outlast Rivals & Beat Par

Longest runner survival time wins each round, plus +2 bonus points for beating the round Par target. Adapt your maze for swift Outriders, armored Juggernauts, and Wind Heralds.
